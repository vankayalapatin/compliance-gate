the product as scenarios, runs, seeds, and demo scripts. That framing is
retired: ComplianceGate is a product a company runs every day (see the
product bar in `AGENTS.md`).

- `PROJECT.md` — the original POC decision record.
- `ROADMAP.md` — the original build plan with demo-day credits and dates.
- `SCENARIO.md` — the original demo seed numbers and story beats.
- `ATTACKS.md` — adversarial supplier attacks, thought process only.
- `AUDIT-DATA.md` — read-only data audit against the product bar.
- `FULL-CYCLE.md` — older full-cycle UI run-book, superseded by `docs/ONE-ORDER-RUNBOOK.md`.
- `PRODUCT.md` — the product view; the live product bar is in `AGENTS.md`.

Frozen. Do not touch these files again. Numbers and policies the company
data carries forward are re-stated in `docs/COMPANY-DATA.md`; this archive
is history only, never a build reference.
